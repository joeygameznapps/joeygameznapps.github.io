The short version

On Time has no accounts and no servers. Your training data stays on your device. The free version shows ads from Google that are never personalized, and Google collects some device data to serve them.

Data that stays on your device

Your sessions, reps, settings and Bat Check results are stored only on your device. We never receive them. Deleting the app removes them, and Setup › Erase all data deletes them at any time.

Advertising

Until you buy Remove Ads, the app shows banner ads from Google AdMob on screens outside the drills. Every ad request is non-personalized: Google picks an ad from the screen it appears on, your general region from your IP address, and the time, not from a profile of you. To serve, measure and protect those ads (for example, limiting how often you see one and preventing fraud), Google may still collect device and app information, approximate location derived from your IP address, ad interactions and diagnostic data. The app does not ask to track you, and nothing in it tracks you across other companies' apps and websites. Only ads rated for general audiences are allowed. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent message asks for your choice before any ad is requested, and you can change it at any time in Setup › Ads › Ad privacy choices. To report an inappropriate ad, use Setup › Ads › Report an ad, or the info mark on the ad itself. Google's use of this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in Google's Privacy Policy.

Purchases

The Remove Ads purchase is handled entirely by Apple. We do not receive your name, email or payment details. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Sharing and protection

Google (advertising, above) and Apple (purchases) are the only third parties that receive any data from the app. Each provides the same or greater protection for that data as this policy describes, under its own privacy policy and its agreements with us. We do not sell data, and we share it with no one else.

Retention, deletion and your choices

The app sends us no data, so there is nothing for us to keep or delete. Your on-device data is deleted with Setup › Erase all data, or by deleting the app. Google keeps ad data under its own retention policy (policies.google.com/technologies/retention). Where Google's consent message applies, you can withdraw consent at any time in Setup › Ads › Ad privacy choices; anywhere, buying Remove Ads stops all ads and ad requests. Apple keeps purchase records under its privacy policy. For any question or deletion request about this app, use the contact form below and we will help.

Children

On Time is a general-audience app used by hitters of all ages. It collects no personal information from anyone, ads are non-personalized for every user and limited to general-audience content, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.

Contact and support

Questions, problems, feature requests or anything about your data: use the contact form or email Business@joemet123.com. Put "On Time" in the subject and you will have a reply within 30 days. Joemet123 LLC is the data controller for this app.
