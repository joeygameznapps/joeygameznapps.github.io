What To Include

Purchases And Refunds

Coin Packs And The Piggy-Bank Unlock Are Sold By Apple Through The App Store. If A Purchase Was Charged But Nothing Arrived, Tell Me The Date And The Last Four Digits Of The Apple Order Number And I Will Look Into It. Refunds Are Issued By Apple, Not By Me: Request One At reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your Data

SimpleSlotz Has No Accounts And No Servers Of Its Own — Your Progress Lives On Your Device And, From Version 1.6.3, In Your Own iCloud. What The App's Ad And Purchase Partners See, And The Choices You Have, Are In The Privacy Policy. To Ask About Or Delete Data Held By Those Partners, Use The Email Link Under The Form, With "Privacy" In The Subject.
