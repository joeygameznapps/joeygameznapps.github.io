Your Weight, Charted Like A Stock

Most Weight Apps Smooth Your Readings Into An Average, Which Hides The One Thing That Matters: The Numbers You Keep Getting Stuck At. WeightStock Charts Your Real Weigh-Ins And Reads Your Own History For The Walls That Keep Turning You Around.

It Needs No Account And Has No Server Of Mine: Your Readings Stay On Your Own Device. It Opens With Sample Data You Can Explore First, And It Is Coming Soon To The App Store.

Sticky Values And Breakthroughs

  • The Weights You Keep Stalling At, Found In Your Own Data: The Ceiling A Regain Tends To Stop Under And The Floor You Keep Bouncing Off.
  • How Many Times Each One Held, How Often You Got Past It, And How Far You Are From The Next One.
  • Log A Reading That Finally Gets Past One, And WeightStock Tells You Which One It Was.
  • Milestones Sized To You, For Every Round Step Of Progress Since You Started.

Your Week, Your Goal, Your Forecast

  • "This Week" Compares Your 7-Day Average With The Week Before, Because One Morning Can Swing A Few Pounds On Water Alone.
  • Set A Goal And A Date, And See The Weekly Pace It Needs Against What You Have Actually Been Doing, And When You'll Get There At This Pace.
  • A Forecast Range On The Chart Shows Where Your Weight Is Likely To Be Over The Next Few Weeks. An Estimate From Your Own Readings, Not A Promise.

Insights A Scale Can't Give You

  • Plateaus, Swings And Speed, Each Sized To Your Own Body And Your Own Day-To-Day Wobble.
  • Your Weekly Rhythm: Which Weekday You Read Heavy Or Light.
  • Tag A Reading (A Salty Meal, Travel, Your Own Tags) And See How Tagged Days Compare.
  • Body Fat, Lean Mass, Waist, Steps, Resting Heart Rate And Blood Pressure Beside Your Weight, From Apple Health Or Your Scale.
  • Candlesticks, Trend Lines And Indicators, With "See It On The Chart" For Every Insight.

Your Data, Your Way

  • Log In Seconds On iPhone Or iPad, From Your Wrist With The Apple Watch App, By Voice With Siri, Or From The Home Screen.
  • Home Screen, Lock Screen And StandBy Widgets.
  • Pounds, Kilograms Or Stones, Losing Or Gaining.
  • Import From Apple Health, Popular Scale And Tracker Apps, Or Any CSV File.
  • Apple Health Sync, And Weigh-Ins Saved Back If You Allow It.
  • Automatic Daily Backups On Your Device, Plus A Backup File You Can Keep Anywhere.
  • Text Follows Your Text Size, And VoiceOver Reads The Chart.
  • Share A Clean Progress Image, With The Option To Hide Your Actual Weight.

WeightStock FAQ

Is WeightStock Free?

Yes. WeightStock Is Free With One Small Banner Ad. WeightStock Pro Is An Optional One-Time Purchase That Removes The Ad And Adds Extras, With A Free 14-Day Trial.

Do I Need An Account?

No. There Is No Account, No Sign-In And No Server Of Mine. Your Readings Stay On Your Device, In Its Own Backups, And In Apple Health If You Choose To Sync.

Do The Ads Track Me?

No. WeightStock Never Asks To Track You, So The Ad Never Gets Your Device's Advertising Identifier, And Every Ad Is Non-Personalised. Your Weight And Health Data Are Never Shared With The Ad. See The Privacy Policy For Details.

Is WeightStock Medical Advice?

No. WeightStock Is A Tracking Tool. Talk To A Doctor Before Starting A Weight-Loss Or Weight-Gain Plan.

How Do I Get Help?

Send A Message From The WeightStock Support Page. I Read Every One.
