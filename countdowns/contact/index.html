Overview

Something not working, a purchase that did not arrive, or an idea for EZ Countdowns? Send a message below and a person will reply.

I answer within 3 business days, and within 30 days for privacy requests.

Send a message

(If you prefer to email, you can do that here.)

What to include

  • Your iPhone, iPad or Apple Watch model and the iOS version (Settings > General > About).
  • What you were doing when it happened, and what you expected instead.
  • A screenshot or screen recording if you have one.

Premium, restores and refunds

EZ Countdowns Premium is sold by Apple through the App Store, as a yearly subscription with a 7-day free trial or as a one-time purchase. It removes all ads and unlocks extra features. Bought it on another device, or reinstalled the app? Use Restore Purchases in the app (Settings > EZ Countdowns Premium); Apple keeps the receipt on your Apple Account. Refunds and subscription changes are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com and in Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions.

Reporting an ad

If an ad in EZ Countdowns is inappropriate, send me a message with a screenshot and what the ad showed. I will report it to Google and block that advertiser where I can.

Your data

EZ Countdowns has no accounts and no servers of mine. Your countdowns stay on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud storage. Messages sent here are used only to answer you. The full privacy policy has the details.
