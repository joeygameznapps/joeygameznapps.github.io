A Satisfying Bubble Popping Game For Kids And Grown-Ups

Bubble Pop Frenzy — Formerly Bubble Pop Master — Is A Satisfying, ASMR-Style Bubble Popping Game On iPhone And iPad, Made For Toddlers And Loved By Grown-Ups Too. Bubbles Drift Across The Screen, A Little Finger Touches One, And It Pops — With A Burst Of Color, A Happy Sound And A Gentle Buzz.

It Doubles As A First Learning Game: Pop The Letters Of The Alphabet In Order, Count From 1 To 20, And Hear Every Color Named Out Loud. There Is No Way To Lose, No Reading Needed, And Every Touch Registers, So It Works For The Very Youngest Players.

Learn The ABCs, Numbers, Colors, Animals And Shapes

  • Alphabet: Pop The Letters A To Z In Order. Each Letter Says Its Name As It Pops, And A Wrong Bubble Just Wiggles And Points The Way.
  • Numbers: Count From 1 To 20, One Pop At A Time, Hearing Every Number Spoken.
  • Colors: Every Bubble Shows Its Color In Big Letters And Says It Out Loud — Eleven Colors, From Red And Blue To Pink, Brown, Black, White And Gray.
  • Animals: 64 Animals, From A Dog And A Cow To An Octopus, A Penguin And A Dinosaur. Pop One And Hear Its Name.
  • Shapes: 18 Shapes To Pop And Name, From Circle, Square And Heart To Hexagon, Crescent And Parallelogram.

Rapid Fire: Endless Bubbles

Rapid Fire Packs The Screen With Racing Bubbles That Refill From Every Side As Fast As They Are Popped. It Never Runs Out, Which Makes It A Favorite Sensory Game For Kids Who Love To Keep Popping — And A Surprisingly Relaxing Fidget Game For Grown-Ups.

Seven Free Ways To Play

  • Free Play — Just Bubbles, Nothing To Get Right.
  • Alphabet — The Letters In Order, A To Z.
  • Numbers — Count To 20.
  • Colors — Pop A Color, Hear Its Name.
  • Animals — Pop An Animal, Hear Its Name.
  • Shapes — Pop A Shape, Hear Its Name.
  • Rounds — A Few Bubbles, Then More Each Round, Against A Clock.

Pick How Many Bubbles Are On Screen: Classic, Rapid Fire, Or Calm — Five Big, Slow Bubbles For The Smallest Hands.

Calm, Sensory-Friendly Play For Autistic Kids And Sensory Seekers

For Autistic Kids And Sensory Seekers, Bubble Pop Frenzy Keeps Things Predictable: Free Play Has No Timers And No Way To Lose, And There Are No Ads, Pop-Ups Or Surprises.

Sound And Vibration Can Each Be Switched Off, The Calm Setting Slows Everything Down To Five Big Bubbles, And Reduce Effects Lightens The Visuals And Saves Battery. Seventeen Backgrounds — Night Sky, Farm, Rainforest, Savanna, Under The Sea, Snow, Space, Christmas And More — Set A Different Mood.

Made For iPhone, iPad And iPhone Duo

Bubble Pop Frenzy Fills Every Screen, From iPhone To The Largest iPad. On iPhone Duo It Fills Both Screens, And When Half Folded The Top Half Becomes A Scoreboard — Pops, Streaks, Records And Timers — While The Bubbles Play On The Bottom.

Bubble Pop Frenzy FAQ

Is Bubble Pop Frenzy Free?

Yes. It Is Free To Download, And All Seven Ways To Play — Free Play, Alphabet, Numbers, Colors, Animals, Shapes And Rounds — Are Free, Along With Four Backgrounds And The Classic And Rapid Fire Bubble Amounts. One Optional Purchase Adds More Backgrounds, Bubble Styles, Pop Sounds And The Calm Setting, And A Free 3-Day Trial Lets You Try It All First.

What Age Is Bubble Pop Frenzy For?

Babies, Toddlers And Preschoolers, Roughly 1 To 5 Years Old, In Apple's Kids Category For Ages 5 And Under. Older Kids And Grown-Ups Enjoy Rapid Fire And Rounds Too.

Does Bubble Pop Frenzy Have Ads?

No. There Are No Ads, No Analytics, No Tracking And No Accounts. The One Purchase, The Rate Button And The Link To Our Other Kids App All Sit Behind A Grown-Up Check.

Is Bubble Pop Frenzy Good For Autistic Kids?

It Is Built To Be Calm And Predictable: Free Play Has No Timers And No Way To Lose, There Are No Ads Or Sudden Pop-Ups, Sound And Vibration Can Each Be Switched Off, And The Calm Setting Slows Everything Down. Settings Sit Behind A 2-Second Hold, So A Favourite Setup Stays Put. It Is A Game Rather Than A Therapy Tool, So Every Child Will Take To It In Their Own Way.

Does It Work Offline?

Yes. Everything Plays With No Internet Connection, Which Makes It A Good Game For Car Rides, Flights And Waiting Rooms.

How Does It Help Toddlers Learn?

In Alphabet And Numbers The Bubbles Must Be Popped In Order, A To Z Or 1 To 20, And Each One Says Its Name Out Loud. In Colors, Animals And Shapes Every Bubble Shows And Says What It Is. There Is No Way To Lose, So Every Pop Is A Small Win.

Can Little Ones Change The Settings By Accident?

Not Easily. The Settings Button Only Opens After A 2-Second Hold, Which A Grown-Up Can Switch Off, And Purchases Need A Grown-Up's Puzzle.

Which Devices Does It Run On?

iPhone And iPad With iOS 17 Or Later, And iPhone Duo, Where The Game Fills Both Screens And A Scoreboard Appears On The Top Half When It Is Half Folded.
