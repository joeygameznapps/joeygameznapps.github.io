Overview

SimpleSlotz is a free social slots game for iPhone and iPad. It has no accounts and no servers of its own: your coins and progress live on your device.

Three third parties can receive data: Google AdMob, which serves the ads that keep the game free; Apple, which processes in-app purchases and subscriptions; and Sentry, which receives crash reports, but only if you turn crash reports on. Everything below explains what each of them sees and the choices you have.

Contact

Questions or requests about your data: Business@joemet123.com. Put "SimpleSlotz" in the subject and you will have a reply within 30 days. Joemet123 LLC is the data controller for this app.

What The App Stores On Your Device

Your coin balance, level, collections, settings, chosen name and avatar are saved only on your device. They are not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them. The game does not ask you to sign in and does not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or location.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

SimpleSlotz shows banner ads and optional rewarded video ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To serve and measure ads, Google's software in the app may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when iOS asks;
  • IP address, approximate location derived from it, device model, operating system and language;
  • which ads were shown, viewed, clicked or watched to completion;
  • diagnostic and performance information about ad delivery.

Google uses this data to serve ads, to measure their performance, to detect fraud and, where you have consented, to personalise the ads you see. Google may share it with its advertising partners. How Google handles this data is described in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Tracking And Your Choices

On first launch, iOS asks whether SimpleSlotz may track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the advertising identifier is not shared and you see non-personalised ads. The game plays exactly the same either way. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings under Privacy & Security, Tracking.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a consent screen appears before any ad is requested. You can decline personalised advertising there and change your choice later from the same screen when it is shown again.

Rewarded ads are always optional. You choose to watch them in exchange for in-game coins; nothing plays unless you tap to watch.

Inside the app, Settings, About The Ads lists what is used to choose an ad and lets you report an inappropriate ad to us by email. We use reports to block advertisers and ad categories in AdMob.

Crash Reports (Sentry, Optional)

Crash reports are off unless you turn them on, either on the "Your choices" card the app shows once or in Settings, Share Crash Reports. While they are on, the app sends crash and error reports and basic game statistics to Sentry, a service of Functional Software, Inc., so we can find and fix bugs.

  • What is sent: the error and where in the app it happened, the app version and build, device model, iOS version and language, your level, a short log of recent in-game events (such as spins, bonuses and screens opened), session start and end, and which version of an in-app test (for example, whether the welcome tour was shown) your install was given.
  • What is never sent: your name or chosen username, your avatar or photo, your coin balance, your advertising identifier, your email, contacts or location.
  • Sentry may record your IP address when a report arrives; it is not used to identify you.

Turn the switch off in Settings at any time and nothing more is sent from that moment. Reports already received are deleted automatically within 90 days, or sooner if you ask us at the address above. Sentry's handling of this data is described in Sentry's Privacy Policy.

In-App Purchases (Apple)

Coin packs, the one-time starter pack, the piggy-bank unlock, No Banner and the VIP subscription are sold through Apple's App Store using Apple's in-app purchase system. Apple processes the payment and holds your payment details; SimpleSlotz never sees your card or account information. The app receives only a confirmation that a purchase completed, which it uses to add coins or unlock the item on your device, and for VIP it reads from Apple, on your device, whether your subscription is active and when it renews or ends. Restore Purchases asks Apple for the same information. Purchases are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy and the App Store terms.

Coins are for play only. They have no cash value, cannot be withdrawn, exchanged or transferred, and are not refundable except as Apple's policies provide.

Notifications

Bonus reminders are off unless you turn them on, on the "Your choices" card or in Settings, Bonus Reminders. When they are on, and iOS notifications are allowed, the app schedules local reminders on your device: one when your timed bonus is ready, and one or two if you have not played for a few days, mentioning the coins waiting for you. They are generated on the device and involve no server and no data leaving your phone. Turning the switch off cancels every pending reminder at once.

Children

SimpleSlotz contains simulated gambling and is intended for adults. It is not directed at children under 17, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. Ads are requested without child-directed treatment.

Your Rights

Because the app keeps your game data only on your device, you control it directly: deleting the app removes it. You can withdraw each consent inside the app at any time: crash reports and bonus reminders with their switches in Settings, and personalised ads with Settings, Privacy Choices (where it applies) or iOS Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking. To have crash reports already sent to Sentry deleted, email us and we will delete them. For data processed by Google AdMob, you can withdraw consent to personalised ads through the tracking setting and, in Europe, the consent screen described above, and you can exercise your rights with Google as described in its privacy policy. If you live in a region with data protection rights such as access, correction or deletion, contact us at the address above and we will help.

How Third Parties Protect Your Data

We share data only with Google AdMob, Apple and, if you turn crash reports on, Sentry, and only for the purposes described above. Each of them is bound by its own privacy policy and data processing terms, which provide the same or equal protection of your data as this policy and as Apple's App Review Guidelines require. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with anyone else, including any parent, subsidiary or related company.

Security And Retention

We do not operate servers that hold your data. Game data stays on your device until you delete the app or reset your progress. Crash reports, if you turned them on, are kept by Sentry for at most 90 days and then deleted automatically. Google retains advertising data under its own policies, and Apple retains purchase records under its own.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with an updated effective date.
