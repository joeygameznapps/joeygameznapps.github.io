A Satisfying Light Switch App For iPhone And iPad

EZ Light Switch Is An ASMR Fidget App That Is Just A Light Switch, And It Is Free To Download. Flip It And Glowing Bulbs Light Up A Living Fish Tank While Colourful Bubbles Burst Out All Around The Switch. Flip It Again And The Room Goes Dark.

There Is No Score, No Timer And No Way To Lose, Just A Switch That Answers The Instant You Touch It, Every Single Time. It Is A Quiet, Calm Fidget For Anyone Who Flips Switches Because It Feels Good, Including Autistic, ADHD And Sensory-Seeking People.

Screenshots

Five Switches To Flip

  • Rocker: A Wide Paddle With A Tiny Pilot Light.
  • Classic Toggle: The Traditional Bat-Handle Wall Switch, Sharp And Loud.
  • Push Button: Press It In And It Lights Up.
  • Pull Chain: Drag The Chain Down, Let Go And Listen To It Ring.
  • Big Lever: A Whole-Arm Lever. Deeply Unnecessary. Very Good.

Two Switches Are There When You Open The App, And The Other Three Open Up As You Flip, All Five Inside Your First Sitting.

Satisfying ASMR Clicks And Haptics

There Are No Recordings In This App. Each Click Is Synthesized From The Way A Real Mechanism Behaves: The Lever Leaves Its Detent, And A Few Milliseconds Later The Contact Slams Home. Each Click Varies Slightly From Flip To Flip. On iPhone, Matching Haptics Make Every Flip Feel As Good As It Sounds.

Six Free Rooms, Six More With Pro

Choose Ocean, Crimson, Workshop, Neon, Dusk Or Emerald From The Row Of Colours At The Bottom Of The Screen, All Free. From Version 2.5, Pro Adds Six More Rooms, Marked With A Padlock Until You Have It: Arctic, Volcano, Deep Sea, Kelp Forest, Shipwreck And A Christmas Room, Twelve In All. Every Room Is A Fish Tank With The Same Crowd Drifting Past: Fish, A Friendly Shark, An Octopus, Jellyfish And A Sea Turtle. The Six Free Rooms Are Reef Tanks, With Sunlight Falling Through The Water And Coral And Sea Grass Growing From A Sandy Floor. The Pro Rooms Swap The Reef For Ice, Volcanic Vents, Deep Dark Water, A Kelp Forest, A Shipwreck And A Decorated Christmas Tree. Real-Looking Glass Bulbs Glow Overhead, With Beams Of Light Falling Through The Water.

Bubbles When The Light Comes On

Every Time The Light Comes On, Colourful Bubbles Burst Out All Around The Switch, And Milestones And Newly Unlocked Switches Get An Even Bigger Burst. With Reduce Motion Turned On, The Tank Holds Still And The Bubbles Stay Away.

Pro And The Apple Watch App

From Version 2.5, EZ Light Switch Pro Is One Purchase, Yours For Good And Shareable With Your Family Sharing Group. It Removes The Ad, Adds The Six Pro Rooms And Unlocks The Apple Watch App, Where A Tap Flips The Switch And The Digital Crown Dims The Light. The Apple Watch App Needs watchOS 26 Or Later And Has No Ads. A Smaller One-Time Remove Ads Purchase Takes Away Only The Banner Ad.

EZ Light Switch FAQ

Is EZ Light Switch Free?

It Is Free To Download And Free To Play. Unless You Have Bought Remove Ads (Or, From Version 2.5, Pro), A Small Banner Ad Runs Along The Top. From Version 2.5, EZ Light Switch Pro Is A One-Time Purchase That Removes The Ad, Adds Six More Rooms And Unlocks The Apple Watch App, And A Smaller One-Time Remove Ads Purchase Removes Only The Ad. Both Can Be Shared With Family Sharing.

What Is EZ Light Switch?

An ASMR Fidget App Built Around Light Switches: Flip A Switch, Light Up A Fish Tank And Pop Colourful Bubbles. There Is No Score, No Timer And No Way To Lose.

Does EZ Light Switch Work Offline?

Yes. Everything Runs On Your Device, So You Can Flip Switches Anywhere Without An Internet Connection. Only Ads And Purchases Need One.

Is EZ Light Switch Good For Autistic Or Sensory-Seeking People?

It Was Designed With Them In Mind: Predictable, Calm, Quiet, With Switches That Answer The Instant You Touch Them. The Light Only Changes When You Flip, And It Fades In And Out Rather Than Cutting. Nothing Times Out, And Reduce Motion Holds The Fish Tank Still And Keeps The Bubbles Away.

Do The Clicks Use Recorded Sounds?

No. Every Click Is Synthesized From How A Real Mechanism Behaves, And Each One Varies Slightly From Flip To Flip. On iPhone, Matching Haptics Make Each Flip Feel As Good As It Sounds.

Which Devices Does EZ Light Switch Run On?

iPhone And iPad Running iOS 26 Or Later. On iPad The Switch And The Fish Tank Fill The Whole Screen. From Version 2.5 There Is Also An Apple Watch App, Included With Pro, For Apple Watch Running watchOS 26 Or Later.

What Does EZ Light Switch Pro Include?

From Version 2.5, Pro Is One Purchase That Removes The Ad, Adds Six More Rooms (Arctic, Volcano, Deep Sea, Kelp Forest, Shipwreck And A Christmas Room) And Unlocks The Apple Watch App, Where The Digital Crown Dims The Light. It Can Be Shared With Family Sharing, And The Six Original Rooms Stay Free.

Do The Ads Track Me?

No. The Ads Are Contextual And Non-Personalised, The App Never Requests The Advertising Identifier And Nothing Is Tracked Across Other Apps. See The Privacy Policy For Details.

How Do I Report An Ad?

If The Ad Shows An AdChoices Icon, Tap It. You Can Also Use The Report An Ad Form On The Support Page. From Version 2.5 The About Screen In The App Also Links To It.
