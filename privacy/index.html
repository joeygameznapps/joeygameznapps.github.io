Joemet123 LLC is a one-person company, so on this page "I", "me" and "my" mean Joemet123 LLC.

The Short Version

This website runs no accounts, logins, analytics or advertising. The only information you hand over is what you type into a support form.

Everything else on this page is about the outside services the site loads, which are described below rather than buried.

What This Website Collects

Nothing automatically. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no newsletter and no analytics package on this site.

If you use a support form, you submit your name, your email address and your message. That form is processed by Web3Forms (web3forms.com), which forwards it to my inbox. Those details are used only to reply to you. They are not sold, and you are not added to any mailing list. Web3Forms handles the message in transit under its own privacy policy.

Services This Website Loads

When you use these, the company behind them can see your IP address. Each operates under its own privacy policy:

  • Web3Forms — processes the support forms, as described above.
  • Apple App Store — download links leave this site for apple.com, under Apple's privacy policy.

No advertising, web fonts, video players or social widgets are loaded here. The text uses your device's own system font.

The Apps Themselves

This policy covers the website only. What an app does with data is set out in that app's own privacy policy, reached from its page on this site and from its App Store listing.

Your Choices And Rights

This site sets no cookies, so there is nothing to block or clear.

If you are in the EU, UK or another region with equivalent rules, you have rights to access, correct or delete personal data held about you. The only thing held is support form email, so write to me and it will be deleted.

Children

This website is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. Some of the apps are made for kids; their own privacy policies cover that. If you believe a child has sent information through a support form, email me and it will be removed.

Changes & Contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the change is described here rather than quietly absorbed.
