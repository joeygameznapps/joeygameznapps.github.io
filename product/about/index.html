Your Day, Scored

ProDuct Turns Your Daily Routine Into A Game You Actually Want To Win. Set Up Your Week, And When A Task Comes Due, Answer It: Complete, Fail Or Skip. Finish Within An Hour Of Its Time And It Pays Full XP.

Most Planners Let You Tick Boxes And Forget Them. ProDuct Holds You To The Time You Set, Then Shows You Your Streaks, Your Won Weeks And How Each Goal Is Going. It Is Free With A Small Banner Ad, With No Account And No Sign-Up. ProDuct Is On The App Store Now.

XP, Levels And Streaks

  • XP For Every Task You Complete, With A Bonus For Doing It On Time.
  • A Perfect-Day Bonus When Every Task Of The Day Is Done.
  • Levels With Their Own Titles, And Every Few Levels Unlock A New Colour Theme.
  • A Day Streak For Days At 80% Or Better, And Win The Week: Hit Your Weekly Target To Grow A Run Of Won Weeks.
  • Rest Days That Never Break Your Streak.

Goals With Their Own Rings

Name A Goal, Or Pick One Like Get Stronger Or Read More, Then Tick The Tasks That Move You Toward It. Each Goal Gets Its Own Ring That Fills With Every Task You Do For It This Month, And Shows How You Compare With Last Month.

Built Around Your Week

  • Temporary Tasks For A Single Day, And Next Week One Swipe Away For Planning Ahead.
  • Reminders That Never Grade You.
  • History By Day, Week, Month And Year, And Habits That Show What You Keep Up And What You Let Slide.

ProDuct FAQ

Is ProDuct Free?

Yes. ProDuct Is Free With A Small Banner Ad. Remove Ads And ProDuct Pro Are Optional One-Time Purchases, Never A Subscription.

How Does The XP Work?

Every Task You Complete Earns XP, And Finishing Within An Hour Of Its Scheduled Time Earns A Bonus. A Day With Every Task Done Earns A Perfect-Day Bonus. Failing A Task Costs Nothing, But Leaving Days Unlogged Does.

Does ProDuct Sync Between My Devices?

Yes. Your Schedule And Progress Sync Through Your Own Private iCloud Account, With No Sign-Up And No Account To Make. They Never Come To Me.

Which Devices Does ProDuct Run On?

iPhone And iPad With iOS 17 Or Later, Apple Watch, And Mac Computers With Apple Silicon.

Do The Ads Track Me?

Only If You Allow Tracking When The App Asks. If You Choose Not To, The App Never Receives Your Device's Advertising Identifier And The Ads Are Chosen From Context Alone. Remove Ads Or ProDuct Pro Removes The Ads Entirely. See The Privacy Policy For Details.

How Do I Get Help?

Send A Message From The ProDuct Support Page. A Person Will Reply, Usually Within 3 Business Days.
