Overview

Something not working, the game running slowly, or an idea for the city? Send a message below and a person will reply.

Put Meatbawl City in the subject line. I answer within 3 business days, and within 30 days for privacy requests.

Send a message

Your name, email and message are sent through Web3Forms to Joemet123 LLC so I can answer you. See the app privacy policy and the site privacy policy.

(If you prefer to email, you can do that here.)

What to include

  • Your iPhone or iPad model and iOS version (Settings → General → About).
  • The game's version, shown in the game under Settings → Version.
  • Where you were in the city, which car, the graphics level, and the weather and time of day.
  • What happened and what you expected instead, with a screenshot or screen recording if you have one.

Sending a performance report

If the game stutters, drops frames or gets hot, a performance report helps most. In the game, open Settings → Share performance report and send the file using the email link under the form. It holds technical measurements only, nothing personal; see the privacy policy.

Your data

Meatbawl City has no accounts and no servers, and collects nothing. The full privacy policy is one page long.
