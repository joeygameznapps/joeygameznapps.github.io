Overview

Something not working, a purchase that did not arrive, an ad that looks wrong, or an idea for EZ Light Switch? Send a message below and a person will reply.

We answer within 3 business days, and within 30 days for privacy requests.

Send a message

Your name, email and message are sent through Web3Forms to us so we can answer you. See the app privacy policy and the site privacy policy.

What to include

  • Your iPhone, iPad or Apple Watch model and its iOS or watchOS version (on iPhone and iPad, Settings → General → About).
  • What you were doing when it happened, and what you expected instead.
  • A screenshot or screen recording if you have one.

Report an ad

The banner ad in EZ Light Switch is served by Google AdMob. If an ad looks inappropriate or out of place, you can report it in either of two ways:

  • If the ad shows an AdChoices icon (a small icon in its corner), tap it. It shows why you are seeing the ad and lets you report it to Google.
  • Tell us using the form below. From version 2.5, the About screen in the app (the information button in the bottom row) has a Report an ad link that brings you straight here.

Please include the date and roughly the time you saw it, what the ad showed or said, and the advertiser or app it was for if that was visible. The form takes text only; if you have a screenshot, email it to us instead. We use reports to block that advertiser or ad category in AdMob, and if you leave an email address we will reply within 3 business days.

Your report, and your email if you add one, are sent through Web3Forms to us so we can act on it. See the app privacy policy and the site privacy policy.

Pro, Remove Ads, restores and refunds

Remove Ads is a one-time purchase that removes the banner ad. From version 2.5 there is also EZ Light Switch Pro, a one-time purchase that removes the ad, adds six more rooms (Arctic, Volcano, Deep Sea, Kelp Forest, Shipwreck and Christmas) and unlocks the Apple Watch app. Both are sold by Apple through the App Store, neither is a subscription, and both are shared with everyone in your Family Sharing group.

Where to find them. Tap the small round button with an X in it, in the bottom row beside the coloured circles. It is there while the app is showing ads, so it disappears once ads are removed. From version 2.5, tapping a room with a padlock opens the same sheet. The sheet shows the prices and the Get Pro, Just remove ads and Restore Purchases buttons (versions before 2.5 show Remove ads and Restore purchase).

Restoring. Bought it on another device, or reinstalled the app? Open the sheet and tap Restore Purchases; Apple keeps the receipt on your Apple Account. Family members get shared purchases by tapping Restore Purchases too. If a purchase was charged but the ads did not go, or Pro did not unlock, tell us the date and the last four digits of the Apple order number and we will look into it. Refunds are issued by Apple, not by us: request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

The Apple Watch app. From version 2.5 it comes with Pro. Install it from the Watch app on your iPhone, or from the App Store on the watch. Without Pro it shows the offer to get Pro, with its own Restore Purchases button. If you bought Pro on your iPhone and the watch still shows the offer, tap Restore Purchases there to check with Apple again.

Your data

EZ Light Switch has no accounts and no servers of ours. Your flip count and chosen room stay on your device, and from version 2.5 the Apple Watch app keeps only its room, dimmer level and Pro unlock on the watch. Purchases are handled by Apple. If you email us or use a form on this page, we receive the name, email address and message you send; the forms are delivered by Web3Forms, which passes them to our inbox. We use them only to answer you and delete them on request. The full privacy policy has the details.
