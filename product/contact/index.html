Overview

Something not working, a purchase that did not arrive, or an idea for ProDuct? Send a message below and a person will reply.

We answer within 3 business days, and within 30 days for privacy requests.

Send a message

What to include

  • Your iPhone or iPad model and iOS version (Settings → General → About).
  • What you were doing when it happened — which tab, which task, or whether it was the widget or Live Activity — and what you expected instead.
  • A screenshot or screen recording if you have one.

Purchases, restores and refunds

Open the Settings tab: the ProDuct Pro and Remove Ads cards show the price and the buy button, with Restore purchase underneath. Both are sold by Apple through the App Store as one-time purchases, and Pro includes Remove Ads. Bought it on another device, or reinstalled the app? Tap Restore purchase; Apple keeps the receipt on your Apple Account. If a purchase was charged but the ads did not go, tell us the date and the last four digits of the Apple order number and we will look into it. Refunds are issued by Apple, not by us: request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Fresh Start. It is in the same Settings tab, with its own card. Once you confirm, the payment goes through Apple and your XP, level and history are cleared; your schedules stay. It is a consumable, so it is used up when applied and is not covered by Restore purchase. If you were charged and nothing was reset, reopen the app first (it finishes an interrupted purchase on launch); if it is still not reset, tell us the date and the last four digits of the Apple order number. Refunds are issued by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

Reporting an ad. The banner ad is served by Google AdMob. Use the small information icon on the ad to see why it was shown or to report it, or email us the date and what the ad showed and we will pass it on.

ProDuct has no accounts and no servers of ours. Your schedule and progress stay on your device (and in your own iCloud if you use it), and Settings has Export, Import and Reset all data. Messages sent here are used only to answer you. The full privacy policy has the details.
